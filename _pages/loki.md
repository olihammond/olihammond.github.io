LoKI is the small-angle neutron scattering instrument at the European Spallation Source. This is an interactive 3D model of it: follow the neutrons from the source, through the spinning choppers and more than 20 metres of beamline, into a sample and onto the detectors. You can walk anywhere, including inside the vacuum tank, and change the instrument settings to see what they do.

It opens as a page of its own so the model can use the whole window. It works best on a computer with a keyboard and mouse: **W A S D** to walk, the mouse to look around, and the number keys **1** to **7** to jump between stations. On a phone, drag the left half of the screen to walk and the right half to look.

The model is illustrative. The layout and sizes are approximated from engineering drawings, the beam is slowed down about 56 times, and the neutrons are drawn far larger than life.
