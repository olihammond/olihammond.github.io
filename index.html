Oliver S. Hammond

Computational Instrument Scientist, European Spallation Source · Affiliated Faculty, KTH · Visiting Fellow, UNSW Sydney

ESS Data Management & Scientific Computing

Copenhagen, Denmark

I am a physical chemist working on the structure of liquids and soft matter. At the European Spallation Source I am the computational instrument scientist for small-angle neutron scattering, based in the Data Management & Scientific Computing division, where I look after the data side of instrument development: reduction pipelines, analysis and commissioning.

My research is on ionic liquids and deep eutectic solvents: how these “sustainable liquids” are structured at the nanoscale, how they self-assemble, and how that structure can be put to work in lubricants, electrolytes and nanoparticle synthesis. The tools are neutron and X-ray scattering, reflectivity and atomistic modelling.

Alongside ESS I hold an adjunct position at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm (Division of Surface and Corrosion Science, with Prof. Mark Rutland), I am a Visiting Fellow in the School of Chemistry at UNSW Sydney, and a LINXS Fellow coordinating the Young Researchers’ Initiative in Soft Matter. Before this I was a postdoc in Aarhus, Stockholm, Lyon and Belfast, after a PhD at the University of Bath and the ISIS Neutron and Muon Source.

The full history is on the CV page, papers are under publications, and any positions I am advertising are listed under openings.
